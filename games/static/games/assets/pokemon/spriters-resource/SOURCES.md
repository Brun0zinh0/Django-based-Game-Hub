## Pokemon Showdown battle animations

- `games/static/games/pokemon_showdown_move_anims.js` — generated by `fetch_showdown_animations.py`
  - Source: https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client @ commit `218cc779512d` (pinned; re-run the script to refresh)
  - Files taken: `play.pokemonshowdown.com/src/battle-animations-moves.ts` (licence header: **CC0-1.0**),
    `battle-animations.ts` tables `BattleOtherAnims` + `BattleEffects` (their header: "The battle
    replay/animation engine (battle-*.ts) by itself is **MIT**"; the client as a whole is AGPLv3,
    but these files are explicitly carved out).

- `games/static/games/assets/pokemon/showdown-fx/` — generated by `fetch_showdown_fx.py`
  - 65 effect images from https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/fx/, enumerated from the embedded
    BattleEffects table (several are opengameart.org pieces credited in the table's comments,
    e.g. the icicle spell). Same battle-engine carve-out as the animation tables above.

- `games/assets/pokemon/capture/balls/` — fetched by `fetch_capture_balls.py`
  - https://github.com/pret/pokefirered `graphics/items/icons/*_ball.png` + `icon_palettes/*_ball.pal`
  - Poke/Great/Ultra/Master item icons with their real JASC palettes applied and index-0
    transparency; each saved closed plus top/bottom halves for the hinged-open pose.
    (raw/hgss-poke-balls.png, asset 233231, stays for reference; its layout is spin
    frames on colour plates and defeated two slicing attempts.)

- `games/assets/pokemon/showdown/front-shiny/` + `back-shiny/` — fetched by `fetch_showdown_shinies.py`
  - https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/ani-shiny/ and ani-back-shiny/ — 481 complete
    animated shiny pairs under the manifest's slugs, unlocked by the Legacy Shop's loot box.
    Pikachu's cap forms have no shiny upstream (none exists in the games either).

- `games/assets/pokemon/audio/battle/` — fetched by `fetch_battle_audio.py`
  - Same Black/White "Sound Effects" rip as the capture cues (asset 487564): the three
    KOUKA effectiveness knocks, DEAD (faint), RECOVERY, LVUP and TOUJOU_MID (send-out).

- `games/assets/pokemon/audio/cries/` — fetched by `fetch_showdown_cries.py`
  - https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/audio/cries/<slug>.mp3 — 1068 cries under the same
    compact slugs the sprite manifest uses (Nidoran's hyphenated sprite name maps onto the
    compact cry name at fetch time). 60 regional/battle forms have no cry upstream and stay
    silent; none are reachable in the three playable leagues.

- `games/assets/pokemon/audio/capture/` — fetched by `fetch_capture_audio.py`
  - https://sounds.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/pokemonblackwhite/asset/487564/ ("Sound Effects")
  - The gen 4/5 SDAT ball cues (SEQ_SE_NAGERU, BOWA2/3, TB_KON, GETTING, SEQ_ME_POKEGET),
    unchanged since Diamond/Pearl; HGSS's own rip on the site has no ball sounds. The 146 MB
    source zip is cached under tmp/ and never committed.

- `raw/frlg-red-back.png` + `games/assets/pokemon/capture/throw/` — sliced by `fetch_trainer_throws.py`
  - Red: https://github.com/pret/pokefirered `graphics/trainers/back_pics/red_back_pic.png` (decomp rip, 5x64x64)
  - Ethan/Lyra: sliced from `raw/hgss-trainers-back.png` (asset 41995). The sheet is two
    characters per labelled band, five frames each; the players are the FIRST band --
    Ethan its left five cells, Lyra its right five -- with identity asserted by palette
    (Ethan's red jacket under a black cap; Lyra's white hat, brown pigtails and slate
    overalls). Earlier cuts fingerprinted island head colours with black treated as
    background, which dissolved the outlines and shipped Barry as "Ethan" and Dawn as
    "Lyra".

- `games/assets/pokemon/ds-particles/` — generated by `extract_hg_particles.py` + `build_ds_particle_moves.py`
  - 1438 particle textures decoded from the owner's own HeartGold ROM (filesystem a/0/2/9, the
    move-particle NARC; SPA texture blocks in A5I3/A3I5, format reversed in the extractor).
    The ROM itself is gitignored and never committed.
  - `moves.json` (518 moves) joins them to move slugs via BluRosie/hg-engine's per-move
    animation scripts (`loadparticle` archive ids) and its `include/constants/moves.h`.

- `ui/hoenn-title.svg` — official Pokemon Emerald wordmark
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pokemon_Emerald.svg
- `ui/johto-title.svg` — hand-authored HGSS-style gold wordmark (no clean 2D rip of the
  HeartGold/SoulSilver logo exists on the archives this project uses).

- `ui/sinnoh-title.svg` — hand-authored Platinum-style wordmark (Johto precedent: no clean
  2D rip of the DPPt logo on the archives this project uses).
- `raw/platinum-poketch.png` + `ui/sinnoh-town-map.png` — Pokétch sheet (asset 20100,
  ripped by Ultra Violet), its Marking Map app cropped as Sinnoh's league map.
- `battle-scenes/sinnoh/` — built by `build_battlegrounds.py` from the same painted
  battleback set and HGSS Location Art as the other regions, on night variants.

- `ui/johto-town-map.png` + `ui/hoenn-town-map.png` — cropped by `fetch_league_maps.py`
  - Johto: https://www.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/pokemonheartgoldsoulsilver/asset/157312/
    ("Full World Map + Objects"; the first left-column panel is the complete Johto/Kanto map)
  - Hoenn: https://www.spriters-resource.com/game_boy_advance/pokemonemerald/asset/116724/
    ("Hoenn Map UI"; the lower third is interface chrome and is cropped away)
